The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants all write the day first. Their dates come out with day and month swapped: booked for 2 December, invite for 12 February. It reaches every channel: app, email, and the booking-v2 form. This is the bug #4471 names, and the one engineering fixed.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The result looks the same as parser, so it was merged, but it only happens on the form, only for tenants on booking-v3, and the parser fix does not touch it. Retiring the form ends it.

shift: a date moves by one day. Not a swap. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” “Every booking in June is one day off.” An invite that arrived before the booking. The desk already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but the cause table on #4471 still counts them under parser by tenant. It looks like a time-zone or DST conversion, and no fix for it is queued. Closing #4471 did not close it: expect more of these.

How to tell them apart

  1. Does the report give two dates that are each other with day and month swapped? If not, and the date moved by a day, it is shift.
  2. A swap that came in through the booking form of a booking-v3 tenant is hint.
  3. Every other swap from a day-first tenant is parser.

Ask the tenant, not intake. Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and on #4471 it disagrees with the tenant in both directions; the table shows by how much.
